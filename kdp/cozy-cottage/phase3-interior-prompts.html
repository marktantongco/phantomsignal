Production notes

Page math — the free 64 pages

KDP's US black-ink paperback schedule is $1.00 fixed + $0.012/page with a $2.30 minimum that covers everything from 24 up to 108 pages. That means a 44-page book and a 108-page book cost the same to print. Single-sided printing — the standard for bold & easy because markers bleed — is therefore free for this book:

InteriorPagesPrint costRoyalty @ $9.99 (60%)
4 front matter + 40 designs, back-to-back44$2.30$3.69
4 front matter + 40 single-sided designs84$2.30 (still under the floor)$3.69
6 front/back matter + 40 single-sided + 6 bonus98$2.30$3.69
…but crossing 108 pages110$2.32 and climbingdrops

Recommendation: ship 4 front matter pages + 40 single-sided designs + blank versos + 2 back matter pages = 86 pages. Under the floor, so it costs the same as the 44-page version, and you get the marker-friendly single-sided interior that the bold & easy buyer expects. Verify against KDP's printing-cost page before you set the price — the schedule changes.

Cut list — if the listing must be a true 40-page book

If your listing promises "40 pages" rather than "40 designs", drop these four and ship 4 front matter + 36 single-sided designs = 76 pages. Every group stays represented.

  • 14. Mixing Bowl - CUT LIST candidate - overlaps page 11 (Baking Day)
  • 24. The Sewing Table - CUT LIST candidate - pins and scissors are the fiddliest shapes in the deck
  • 28. The Beehive - CUT LIST candidate if you need a fourth cut
  • 36. The Washstand - CUT LIST candidate - lowest emotional pull of the group

Front matter (4 pages, no prompts needed)

  • p1 Half title / title page — reuse the page 1 cottage as a small centered vignette, 40% size
  • p2 "This book belongs to" — two ruled lines, one small tulip
  • p3 How to use — 3 short lines: single-sided, thick lines, any medium
  • p4 Color test page — six empty swatch circles

Generation workflow

  • 1. Generate page 1 first. Iterate until it is exactly right. That image becomes your style reference.
  • 2. Feed page 1 back as an image reference / style reference for pages 2–40. This is the single biggest consistency lever you have.
  • 3. Batch by group, 8 at a time, so drift is easy to spot.
  • 4. Upscale to 3000 × 3000 px minimum (8.5 in at 300 DPI = 2550 px; 3000 gives you bleed headroom).
  • 5. Cleanup: convert to grayscale, levels/threshold to crush gray to pure white, then check that no line is thinner than ~12 px at 300 DPI (about 1 mm printed).
  • 6. Assemble at 8.75 × 8.75 in with 0.125 in bleed; keep all art inside a 0.5 in safe margin.

Per-model parameters

  • Midjourney: append --ar 1:1 --style raw; use --sref pointing at page 1 and --cref if you want a recurring cat. --style raw matters — the default MJ look adds shading you do not want.
  • Ideogram: strongest at clean line art and best at obeying "no text"; use its Magic Prompt off and paste the full locked prompt verbatim.
  • Flux / SDXL: paste the negative lock into the negative field instead of the prompt tail, and drop guidance to ~3.5 so it stops adding texture.

The five AI traps in this deck

  • Invented text — pages 8, 10, 19, 21, 27. Jars, books, parcels, seed packets and picture frames all come back with gibberish writing. Regenerate; do not Photoshop it out.
  • Gray "glow" — pages 4 and 38. Ask for "unfilled white windows with thick outlines".
  • Wispy smoke and steam — pages 2, 12, 22. Must be thick closed outlines.
  • Micro-spots — page 31 mushroom caps. Small dots are the #1 "this isn't bold and easy" review complaint.
  • Quilt grids — page 33. Ask for "6 large patchwork squares", not a patchwork quilt.

QA gate — every page must pass all six

  • One subject, centered, breathing room on all four sides
  • Line weight visibly identical to page 1
  • Pure black lines on pure white — zero gray pixels
  • No text, no numbers, no watermark
  • Nothing important within 0.5 in of any trim edge
  • Colorable in under 5 minutes with a chisel marker
